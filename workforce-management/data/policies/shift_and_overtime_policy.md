## 1. Shift Classifications & Timings

To ensure 24/7 client coverage and infrastructure resilience, InnovateCorp operates the following standard shifts:

| Shift Name | Shift Code | Timing | Core Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General Shift** | `SFT01` | 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM | Core engineering, product management, and corporate business |
| **Morning Shift** | `SFT02` | 06:00 AM – 03:00 PM | APAC early market coverage & customer operations |
| **Afternoon Shift** | `SFT03` | 02:00 PM – 11:00 PM | EMEA overlap, incident support, and client deployments |
| **Night Shift** | `SFT04` | 10:00 PM – 07:00 AM | 24x7 Mission-critical infrastructure & US time-zone coverage |

---

## 2. Shift Allowances & Benefits

### 2.1 Night Shift Allowance
* Employees assigned to the Night Shift (`SFT04`) receive a differential allowance of **₹500 per completed night shift**, disbursed automatically via monthly payroll.
* **Safe Transport Provision**: Female employees assigned to evening or night shifts departing after 08:00 PM or arriving before 06:00 AM are provided free, GPS-monitored, company-contracted cab transportation with security escorts.

### 2.2 Weekend Shift Allowance
* Scheduled shifts on Saturdays or Sundays receive an additional premium of **₹750 per shift** or a compensatory off, subject to department policy.

## 3. Overtime Policy & Fatigue Management

### 3.1 Eligibility & Prior Authorization
* Overtime is applicable to designated non-exempt engineering, operations, and support staff.
* All overtime work **must be pre-authorized** by the department manager before being performed. Unapproved overtime will not be processed for compensation.

### 3.2 Overtime Compensation Rates
* **Weekday Overtime**: Compensated at **1.5 times the hourly basic pay rate** for every completed hour worked beyond standard 8-hour shift duration.
* **Weekend / Public Holiday Overtime**: Compensated at **2.0 times the hourly basic pay rate**.

### 3.3 Fatigue Mitigation & Safety Limits
* To protect employee health and mental wellness, our AI Shift Allocation engine strictly enforces the following hard constraints:
  * Maximum daily overtime: **3 hours**.
  * Maximum weekly overtime: **16 hours**.
  * Mandatory rest between shifts: A minimum of **12 continuous hours of off-duty rest** must elapse before an employee can be scheduled for another shift.
  * Maximum consecutive night shifts: No employee may be scheduled for more than **5 consecutive night shifts** without a mandatory 48-hour rest break.

---

## 4. Shift Swaps & Schedule Changes
* **Shift Swap Protocol**: Employees may exchange shifts with a qualified peer possessing identical skill qualifications via the portal (`/shifts/swap`).
* Swaps must be submitted at least **24 hours prior** to shift commencement and require mutual peer confirmation followed by manager approval.
