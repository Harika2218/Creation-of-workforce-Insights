# InnovateCorp Hybrid & Remote Work Policy

**Document ID**: POL-HR-003  
**Effective Date**: January 15, 2026  
**Category**: Workplace Flexibility  
**Applicability**: Eligible Knowledge Workers & Technology Roles  
**Version**: 2.1  

---

## 1. Principles of Flexible Work
InnovateCorp embraces modern workplace flexibility through our **Hybrid Work Model**. We balance the benefits of focused remote productivity with in-person collaboration, cross-functional synergy, and mentorship.

---

## 2. Hybrid Schedule & Eligibility

### 2.1 Standard 3:2 Hybrid Framework
* Eligible employees are permitted to work remotely up to **2 days per working week**, with the remaining **3 days based in the assigned physical office location**.
* Department managers have discretion to designate specific in-office anchor days (e.g., Tuesdays and Thursdays) to facilitate team sprint plannings and all-hands reviews.

### 2.2 Roles Ineligible for Remote Work
* Roles requiring physical on-site presence—such as Data Center Infrastructure, Physical Security, Facilities, and Office IT Support—are not eligible for regular remote work.

### 2.3 Probationary Guidelines
* Employees in their first 90 days of employment are expected to work 100% on-site to accelerate onboarding, cultural assimilation, and hands-on peer training, unless an exception is authorized by the Department Head.

---

## 3. Remote Working Requirements & Code of Conduct

1. **Working Hours & Availability**: Remote work days do not alter core business hours. Employees must remain active on company communication channels (Slack/Teams/Email) between 10:00 AM and 04:00 PM.
2. **Dedicated Workspace**: Employees must maintain a quiet, ergonomically sound, and secure physical workspace free from disruptive background noise.
3. **Information Security & Confidentiality**:
   * Remote connection must be routed through the company Virtual Private Network (VPN).
   * Use of unauthorized personal USB storage drives or public unencrypted Wi-Fi hotspots without VPN is strictly prohibited.
   * Work must be executed strictly on company-provisioned secure laptops; non-employees must never be permitted access to company equipment.

---

## 4. Equipment Stipend & Infrastructure Support
* **Home Office One-Time Setup Allowance**: InnovateCorp reimburses up to ₹15,000 for approved ergonomic furniture, external monitors, or webcams upon submission of valid receipts within 60 days of joining.
* **Monthly Internet Subsidy**: All eligible remote-working employees receive a monthly internet allowance of ₹1,500 automatically credited through payroll.

---

## 5. Ad-Hoc WFH Requests & Revocation
* Employees requesting temporary ad-hoc remote days (e.g., due to mild illness or home repairs) must apply through the portal at least 24 hours in advance.
* Remote work privilege is contingent upon sustained high performance (minimum 3.0 performance score) and punctuality. Abuse of remote work privileges or lack of responsiveness during core hours may result in immediate revocation of hybrid eligibility.
