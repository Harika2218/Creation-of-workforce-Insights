# InnovateCorp Code of Business Conduct & Ethics

**Document ID**: POL-HR-005  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Category**: Corporate Governance & Integrity  
**Applicability**: All Board Members, Officers, and Employees Worldwide  
**Version**: 4.0  

---

## 1. Core Principles & Ethical Commitment
InnovateCorp conducts all enterprise affairs with uncompromising honesty, integrity, mutual respect, and regulatory compliance. Every employee serves as a custodian of the company’s reputation.

---

## 2. Workplace Respect & POSH (Anti-Harassment) Policy

### 2.1 Zero-Tolerance for Harassment & Discrimination
InnovateCorp strictly prohibits discrimination or harassment based on race, color, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, marital status, age, or socioeconomic background. All workplace decisions—including hiring, promotion, appraisal, and remuneration—are strictly meritocratic.

### 2.2 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
* In strict compliance with statutory POSH frameworks, an **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** is constituted at every office location, chaired by a senior woman leader.
* Any aggrieved individual may submit a confidential complaint directly to `posh@innovatecorp.demo` or via the anonymous portal within 90 days of an incident.
* The ICC conducts unbiased inquiries within 90 days and maintains strict confidentiality. Retaliation against any complainant or witness is a zero-tolerance offense resulting in immediate summary dismissal.

---

## 3. Conflict of Interest & Secondary Employment
* Employees must avoid situations where personal or financial interests conflict with corporate duties.
* **Secondary Employment (Moonlighting)**: Full-time employees may not accept outside commercial employment, consulting engagements, or directorships with competing technology entities without prior written sanction from the Chief People Officer.

---

## 4. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property
* All inventions, software algorithms, proprietary source code, synthetic data, client lists, and technical documentation developed during employment are exclusive property of InnovateCorp.
* Strict non-disclosure obligations persist even after separation of employment.

---

## 5. Whistleblower Protection & Grievance Redressal
* Employees who observe accounting fraud, bribery, security breaches, or regulatory non-compliance are urged to file a confidential report with the Ethics Hotline (`ethics@innovatecorp.demo`).
* InnovateCorp provides complete immunity from adverse retaliation for all good-faith disclosures.
