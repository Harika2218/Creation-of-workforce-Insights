**Document ID**: POL-HR-007

**Category**: Learning & Development

---

## 1. Commitment to Continuous Learning
InnovateCorp actively invests in workforce upskilling to maintain cutting-edge technological mastery in cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, software engineering, and strategic leadership.

---

## 2. Professional Certification & Training Reimbursement

### 2.1 Annual L&D Allowance
* Each confirmed employee is eligible for an annual **Learning & Development Budget of up to ₹50,000 per fiscal year** for approved external certifications, professional courses, and technical conferences.
* Eligible certifications include AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA), PMP, and specialized AI/ML credentials.

### 2.2 Approval & Reimbursement Protocol
* Courses must be pre-approved by the department manager and HR L&D Partner prior to enrollment.
* Reimbursement is credited within 30 days upon submission of the completion certificate and payment receipts.
* **Service Retention Commitment**: If reimbursement exceeds ₹30,000, a minimum 12-month service tenure post-certification is required. Voluntary resignation prior to 12 months requires pro-rated reimbursement return.

---

## 3. Mandatory Compliance Training
All employees must complete mandatory annual compliance modules within 30 days of release:
1. **Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Awareness** (Annual)
2. **Information Security & Data Privacy (GDPR / ISO 27001)** (Annual)
3. **Anti-Bribery & Code of Conduct Ethics** (Annual)

---

## 4. AI-Powered Skill Gap Recommendations
Our workforce intelligence platform automatically computes departmental and individual skill gap matrixes, recommending targeted internal courses from the company catalog (e.g., *Advanced Python*, *React Architecture*, *FastAPI Microservices*, *Machine Learning Engineering*) directly through `/skills-training` and the AI HR Assistant.
