# InnovateCorp Global Employee Leave Policy

**Document ID**: POL-HR-001  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Category**: Time & Attendance  
**Applicability**: All Full-Time, Part-Time, and Contractual Employees  
**Version**: 3.2

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy establishes the rules, accrual guidelines, eligibility requirements, and procedures governing all statutory and non-statutory leaves of absence at InnovateCorp. The objective is to support employee health, well-being, work-life harmony, and personal responsibilities while ensuring business operational continuity.

---

## 2. Leave Categories & Annual Entitlements

### 2.1 Annual Privilege Leave (PL)
* **Entitlement**: 18 working days per calendar year, accrued proportionately at the rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service.
* **Purpose**: Vacation, personal recreation, rest, and extended planned travel.
* **Accrual & Carry Forward**: A maximum of 12 unused Annual Leave days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Any excess accrued balance beyond 12 days at year-end is automatically encashed at the basic salary rate or forfeited in accordance with statutory guidelines.
* **Notice Requirement**: Must be applied at least 5 business days in advance for requests exceeding 3 consecutive days, and requires direct manager approval.

### 2.2 Casual Leave (CL)
* **Entitlement**: 8 working days per calendar year, credited at the beginning of each year.
* **Purpose**: Urgent, unforeseen personal errands, family commitments, or short personal matters.
* **Usage Limits**: Cannot be availed for more than 3 consecutive days at a time. Casual leave cannot be combined with Annual Privilege Leave.
* **Lapse**: Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the following calendar year and lapses automatically on December 31.

### 2.3 Sick & Medical Leave (SL)
* **Entitlement**: 10 working days per calendar year.
* **Purpose**: Personal illness, medical treatment, quarantine, or temporary physical incapacity.
* **Medical Certification**: If Sick Leave exceeds 2 consecutive working days, an official medical practitioner certificate and fitness certificate upon return must be uploaded to the HR portal.
* **Accumulation**: Unused Sick Leave may accumulate up to a maximum cap of 30 days over consecutive years of service.

### 2.4 Maternity Leave
* **Entitlement**: 26 weeks (182 calendar days) of fully paid leave for female employees for up to two surviving children, in strict compliance with the Maternity Benefit Amendment Act. For third child onwards, entitlement is 12 weeks.
* **Eligibility**: Applicable to female employees who have completed at least 80 days of continuous service in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.
* **Application Notice**: Written notice must be submitted at least 8 weeks prior to the anticipated delivery date along with a registered gynecologist's certificate.
* **Additional Provisions**: Upon return, nursing breaks (two daily breaks until child reaches 15 months) and optional transition to remote work for up to 6 months subject to mutual managerial agreement.

### 2.5 Paternity Leave
* **Entitlement**: 10 consecutive working days of fully paid leave for male employees upon the birth or legal adoption of a child under one year of age.
* **Window of Usage**: Must be availed within 6 months from the date of delivery or legal placement.
* **Notice Requirement**: Written notification provided at least 2 weeks in advance where practicable.

### 2.6 Bereavement Leave
* **Entitlement**: Up to 5 consecutive working days of paid leave upon the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).
* **Additional Travel Days**: Up to 2 additional days may be granted by HR if inter-state or international travel is necessitated.

### 2.7 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
* **Entitlement**: Granted on a 1:1 basis when an employee is explicitly directed by their department manager to work on an official public holiday or scheduled weekly off.
* **Validity**: Must be utilized within 60 calendar days from the date of accrual, after which it expires without monetary compensation.

## 3. Leave Application & Approval Workflow

1. **Submission**: Employees must submit leave requests digitally via the HR Automation Portal (`/leave`) indicating leave type, start date, end date, and reason.
2. **Manager Review**: The direct manager must review and act upon (Approve or Reject) the request within 48 hours of receipt.
3. **Escalation**: Unanswered requests are automatically escalated to the Department Head and HR Business Partner.
4. **Cancellation**: An employee may withdraw or cancel an approved leave before the start date. For retroactive adjustments, HR Admin approval is required.
5. **Rejection & Appeals**: In the event of a leave rejection due to critical project deliverables, the manager must provide explicit written justification in the portal and propose alternative dates.

---

## 4. General Leave Rules & Restrictions
* **Probationary Period**: New employees undergoing the 90-day probationary period are eligible to avail Casual Leave and Sick Leave on a pro-rata basis. Privilege leave accrues during probation but can only be availed post-confirmation.
* **Leave Without Pay (LWP)**: Can only be requested once all accrued paid leave balances have been completely exhausted. Requires HR Director written sign-off.
* **Absconding & Abandonment**: Unapproved absence exceeding 5 consecutive business days without communication is classified as voluntary job abandonment and initiates disciplinary proceedings.
