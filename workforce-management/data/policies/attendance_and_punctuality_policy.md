# InnovateCorp Attendance and Punctuality Policy

**Document ID**: POL-HR-002  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Category**: Time & Attendance  
**Applicability**: All Employees Across All Locations  
**Version**: 2.4  

---

## 1. Overview & Work Hours
Consistent attendance and punctuality are fundamental expectations of employment at InnovateCorp. Regular operational availability ensures optimal team collaboration, client service level adherence, and project milestone fulfillment.

* **Standard Business Hours**: 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM (Monday through Friday), inclusive of a 60-minute combined lunch and refreshment interval.
* **Core Working Hours**: 10:00 AM to 04:00 PM is designated as mandatory core team overlap time during which all employees must be reachable and operational.
* **Minimum Daily Hours for Full-Day Attendance**: 8 hours of logged active work time.
* **Half-Day Attendance**: A minimum of 4 hours but less than 8 hours logged during a scheduled working day.

---

## 2. Check-In Grace Period & Late Arrival Guidelines

### 2.1 Grace Period
* InnovateCorp provides a **15-minute grace period** for morning arrival (up to 09:15 AM for standard general shift). Check-ins between 09:01 AM and 09:15 AM are deemed on-time.

### 2.2 Late Arrival Rules
* Check-in between 09:16 AM and 10:30 AM is categorized as **Late Arrival**.
* **Monthly Allowance**: Employees are permitted up to **3 late arrivals per calendar month** without disciplinary impact.
* **Penalty Threshold**: The 4th late arrival and every subsequent late arrival in the same calendar month will result in a deduction of half a day of Casual Leave (or Leave Without Pay if CL balance is exhausted).
* Arrival after 10:30 AM without prior managerial authorization is automatically logged as a Half-Day Absence.

### 2.3 Early Departure
* Leaving work prior to 05:00 PM (or completing less than 7 hours) requires prior manager approval and a formal regularization submission in the HR portal.

---

## 3. Clock-In / Clock-Out Verification Methods

Employees must record their daily attendance through the authorized company channels:
1. **HR Automation Web Portal / Mobile Verification**: Digital geofenced check-in within designated office geofence radius (500 meters).
2. **Biometric Terminals**: Physical turnstiles and optical scanners installed at company reception gates.
3. **Automated Anomaly Detection**: Our AI workforce intelligence system monitors check-in/out patterns using Isolation Forest anomaly detection to identify erratic clock patterns, missing check-outs, or timestamp discrepancies.

---

## 4. Attendance Regularization
* If an employee forgets to clock in or out, experiences technical hardware malfunction, or is on outdoor client duty, an **Attendance Regularization Request** must be submitted via `/attendance` within **48 hours**.
* Every regularization request must specify the accurate arrival/departure times and mandatory justification.
* Approval by the direct reporting manager is required. Each employee is permitted a maximum of **3 regularizations per month**.

---

## 5. Unscheduled Absences & Punctuality Violations
* Any absence not applied and approved in advance is classified as **Unscheduled Absence**.
* Employees must notify their immediate supervisor by phone or email no later than 09:00 AM on the day of unscheduled absence.
* Habitual tardiness or three consecutive days of unexcused absence triggers an automatic HR review and formal Counseling Notice.
