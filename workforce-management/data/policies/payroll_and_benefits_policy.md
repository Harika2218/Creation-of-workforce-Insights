# InnovateCorp Payroll, Compensation & Benefits Policy

**Document ID**: POL-HR-008  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Category**: Total Rewards  
**Applicability**: All Salaried and Hourly Personnel  
**Version**: 3.5  

---

## 1. Salary Disbursement & Pay Cycle
* **Salary Pay Day**: Net monthly salary is directly credited via Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT/RTGS) on the **last business day of every calendar month**. If the last day is a bank holiday, credit occurs on the immediate preceding working day.
* **Pay Calculation Period**: 1st through the last calendar day of the month.
* **Digital Payslips**: Available electronically for viewing and download in the HR Automation Portal under `/payroll` within 24 hours of payroll execution.

---

## 2. Salary Structure & Deductions

### 2.1 Fixed Compensation Components
* **Basic Pay**: 40% – 50% of Total Cost to Company (CTC).
* **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 50% of Basic Pay for metro locations; 40% for non-metro locations.
* **Special Allowance / Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP)**: Balancing component accommodating tax-exempt allowances (medical reimbursements, LTA, books and periodicals).

### 2.2 Statutory Deductions & Contributions
* **Provident Fund (PF)**: Statutory 12% deduction from Basic Pay with equal matching 12% employer contribution remitted to the Employees' Provident Fund Organization (EPFO).
* **Professional Tax (PT)**: Deducted monthly in accordance with applicable state government statutes.
* **Income Tax (TDS)**: Calculated and deducted on an estimated annualized basis according to employee tax regime selection (Old vs. New Tax Regime).

---

## 3. Health & Welfare Benefits

### 3.1 Group Medical Health Insurance (GMC)
* **Coverage**: All full-time employees, legal spouse, and up to 2 dependent children are covered under a comprehensive cashless hospitalization policy up to **₹5,00,000 per family per policy year**.
* Optional parental insurance top-up is available with subsidized corporate premiums.

### 3.2 Group Personal Accident (GPA) & Term Life
* Life insurance coverage equal to **3x annual fixed CTC** provided at 100% company-paid premium.

---

## 4. Payroll Discrepancy Redressal
Employees detecting any discrepancy in allowances, overtime calculations, or tax deductions must raise a ticket via `payroll@innovatecorp.demo` within 7 business days of payslip publication. Confirmed corrections will be adjusted in the immediate subsequent payroll run.
