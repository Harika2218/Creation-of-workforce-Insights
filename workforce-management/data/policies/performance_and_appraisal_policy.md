## 1. Overview & Framework
InnovateCorp operates an objective, transparent, and continuous Performance Management System designed to align individual contributions with strategic business milestones.

---

## 2. Appraisal Cycle & Evaluation Milestones

* **Annual Appraisal Review**: Conducted during Q4 of each fiscal year (October – December), culminating in annual compensation revisions and promotions effective January 1.
* **Quarterly Milestone Check-Ins (Q1, Q2, Q3)**: Mid-year alignment sessions between employees and managers to review Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), unblock challenges, and calibrate skill development.

---

## 3. Rating Scale & Multi-Factor Scoring

Performance is assessed across a balanced scorecard combining quantifiable goals, behavioral competencies, and operational consistency:

| Rating Band | Descriptor | Definition | Expected Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5.0** | *Exceptional (Role Model)* | Consistently surpasses all performance metrics; recognized industry or company-wide impact. | ~10% |
| **4.0** | *Exceeds Expectations* | Frequently outperforms targets; demonstrates leadership and cross-functional value. | ~25% |
| **3.0** | *Meets Expectations* | Reliable performance fully achieving documented goals and role standards. | ~50% |
| **2.0** | *Needs Improvement* | Partially achieves objectives; inconsistent quality or missed deliverables. | ~10% |
| **1.0** | *Unsatisfactory* | Consistently fails to meet minimum role benchmarks; immediate corrective action required. | ~5% |

### 3.1 Transparent AI Productivity Scoring
* Our AI Workforce Intelligence suite computes transparent, explainable 0–100 scorecards based on 4 verified components:
  1. **KPI Achievement** (30% weight)
  2. **Goal Completion** (25% weight)
  3. **Billable Timesheet Efficiency** (25% weight)
  4. **Attendance Reliability** (20% weight)
* Every employee can inspect their own transparent score components directly in the portal.

---

## 4. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Guidelines
* When an employee receives a 2.0 or 1.0 rating, or displays persistent performance shortfalls, a structured **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** is instituted.
* **Duration**: The standard PIP timeline is **60 calendar days**.
* **Support**: Specific measurable weekly targets, dedicated mentoring, and bi-weekly milestone check-ins are mandated.
* **Outcomes**:
  * Successful completion: Employee returns to good standing.
  * Inconclusive / Failed completion: May result in role realignment or mutually agreed separation.
