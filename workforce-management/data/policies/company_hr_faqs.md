## 1. Leave & Time-Off FAQs

### Q1: How do I apply for leave?
**Answer**: Log into the HR Automation portal, navigate to **Leave Management** (`/leave`), select **Apply Leave**, choose your leave category (Annual, Sick, Casual, etc.), select the date range, enter the reason, and click **Submit**. Your manager will receive an automated notification and must approve within 48 hours.

### Q2: What happens if I fall sick on a scheduled working day?
**Answer**: Notify your direct manager by 09:00 AM. Apply for Sick Leave (SL) in the portal on the day you return. If your illness lasts more than 2 consecutive days, upload a registered doctor's medical fitness certificate.

### Q3: Can I encash unused leave days?
**Answer**: Yes. At calendar year-end, any Annual Privilege Leave exceeding the 12-day carry-forward ceiling can be encashed as part of the January payroll cycle at the basic pay rate. Casual leave cannot be encashed.

## 2. Attendance & Shift FAQs

### Q4: What is the grace period for morning check-in?
**Answer**: A 15-minute grace period applies to all morning shifts (e.g., check-in up to 09:15 AM for standard general shift). Check-ins between 09:16 AM and 10:30 AM count as late arrivals. You receive 3 free late arrivals per calendar month.

### Q5: How do I request a shift swap?
**Answer**: Navigate to **Shifts & Schedules** (`/shifts`), select **Shift Swap Request**, select the target shift and the colleague you wish to swap with. Once your colleague accepts, your reporting manager reviews and approves the change.

## 3. Remote Work & Flexibility FAQs

### Q6: Can I work from home permanently?
**Answer**: InnovateCorp follows a 3:2 Hybrid model (3 days in-office, 2 days remote). Permanent 100% remote arrangements require written approval from the Department Head and Chief People Officer and are reserved for exceptional medical or geographic situations.

### Q7: How do I claim the home internet reimbursement?
**Answer**: The monthly internet allowance of ₹1,500 is automatically included in your monthly payroll. No separate receipt upload is required.

---

## 4. Payroll, Payslips & Benefits FAQs

### Q8: When will my salary be credited?
**Answer**: Salaries are credited directly to your registered bank account on the **last business day of each calendar month**. Digital payslips are available for download under `/payroll` within 24 hours.

### Q9: How do I download my monthly payslip?
**Answer**: Navigate to `/payroll`, select the relevant month from the history dropdown (e.g., `2026-03`), and click **Download PDF** or **View Payslip**.

### Q10: How does the company health insurance work?
**Answer**: All employees and their immediate families are covered up to ₹5,00,000 for cashless hospitalization under our Group Medical policy. Show your Medi-Card (accessible on the portal) at any network hospital.

---

## 5. Resignation & Notice Period FAQs

### Q11: What is the mandatory notice period upon resignation?
**Answer**: The standard notice period for confirmed full-time employees is **60 calendar days (2 months)**. For employees still under initial 90-day probation, the notice period is **30 calendar days**. Notice buyout requires mutual agreement and written Department Head sign-off.
